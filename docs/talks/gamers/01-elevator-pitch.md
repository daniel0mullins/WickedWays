# The Elevator Pitch — for gamers (30–60 seconds)

> Use verbatim or trim to taste. The 30-second core is the first paragraph;
> the second paragraph upgrades it to 60 seconds.

---

Wicked Ways is a co-op horror RPG: your party explores a haunted house one
turn at a time, managing Health, Sanity, and Energy while the house works on
taking all three. You can play it in a browser, as a desktop app, online
with friends — or on a **real, physical board**: e-ink tiles that repaint as
you explore, pieces the board recognizes when you set them down, and your
own dice. And here's the thing — it's the *same game* everywhere. Not a
port. Not a companion app. One rules brain runs every version, so nothing
ever plays differently, no matter where you're playing.

Roll your own d20 for an attack and the game accepts *your* roll — and the
session is still perfectly replayable afterward, because every roll gets
written into the game's ledger. Saves never break. Nobody can cheat, because
every move goes through the same referee. And campaigns are just readable
text files — the campaign we ship, Hollow House, is written exactly the way
a fan-made one would be. The goal, out loud: to be **as moddable as DOOM** —
a game its community keeps feeding for thirty years.

---

**If you get one follow-up question:**

- *"When can I play on the physical board?"* → The brains of it are done and
  tested — the same game drives an on-screen simulator of the board today,
  and the hardware side is in prototyping.
- *"Can I write a campaign without knowing how to code?"* → Yes. A campaign
  is a text file you can read: rooms, doors, monsters, dialogue. If you can
  write a forum post, you can write a haunted house.
- *"Will mods break my save?"* → No. A broken campaign refuses to load with
  an error — it never gets far enough to touch a save.

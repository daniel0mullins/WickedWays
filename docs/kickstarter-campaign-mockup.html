I

Three ways it gets under your skin

◈

The art lives on the table

A single large color e-ink panel is the board's floor — the map is a real object you gather around, not a glow you each stare into alone. Your phone becomes a private whisper: your own sanity, your own afflictions.

☾

Darkness is real

E-ink is reflective, so an unlit room is physically unreadable until light is brought. A dimmable frontlight lets the engine raise the lantern and let it gutter — the table actually goes dark when the story does.

⛧

Every die is obeyed

Roll your own physical dice into the tray and the engine takes the face you rolled as law — or let the house roll for you. To-hit, crits, the critical-miss stumble: yours to cast, the engine's to honor.
